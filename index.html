Job hunting is episodic. Your career shouldn’t be.

Three ways FinanceGigs keeps you continuously matched to your market.

Find

Talent syncing, not job searching. Individually matched roles from thousands of corporate job sites and hiring teams, on your own dashboard and in a weekly email of your top 25. Every role is matched to where you live and want to work, then scored against your function, career level, certifications, systems and work type, so you only hear about jobs that fit. No more sifting through listings: your matches update themselves as the market moves.

Apply

Every role takes you straight to the employer’s own job page. Review it there, and if it fits, apply directly with them.

Roles we find

We read job listings from thousands of corporate career sites every day to bring you the best options: remote roles, and onsite or hybrid roles within commuting distance of where you live. No job board in the middle, no reposted listing, no third-party recruiters calling you. We take you directly to the source.

Roles sent to you directly

Employers who post with us pay to send their role to the FinanceGigs members it fits best, and you hear about it the moment it goes out. Reply INTERESTED and your profile and resume go straight to someone who is actively hiring. No ghost listings, no wasted time.

Know

Critical market insights from reports built around your needs, so you’re making the most informed decisions about where your career goes next.

  • Hiring Trends Market Report, monthly: who’s hiring, at what level and for which skills, across accounting, FP&A, treasury, tax and audit, drawn from thousands of job postings.
  • News Insights, weekly: a curated read on the topics you follow, from accounting standards and audit rules to tax changes and the CFO agenda, drawn from thousands of news outlets.

Not looking? That’s the point.
