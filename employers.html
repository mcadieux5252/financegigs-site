Hiring shouldn’t mean sorting a pile.

A single job board posting can pull in hundreds or thousands of applicants. Applicant Search 2.0 starts from the other end: the right people first.

The old way

Post it and hope.

  • Your role sits on job boards, company sites and search engines next to thousands of others.
  • Anyone can apply with one click, so the pile grows faster than you can read it.
  • Keyword filters guess at fit and miss good people who describe their work differently.
  • Your team spends days screening before a single real conversation.
Applicant Search 2.0

Match first, then talk.

  • Your job description is matched to structured profile data: location, finance functions, career level, skills, certifications, systems, expertise and career goals.
  • Only members who clear your match score get the role, sent straight to them. Nobody else sees it.
  • Every member opted in and wants to hear about roles like yours.
  • Members who want it say so, and their profile and resume come straight to you.
