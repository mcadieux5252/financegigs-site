Questions

Where does the pay data come from?

From the pay ranges employers post on finance roles on their own career sites, which we pull in every hour. Pay transparency laws mean more employers post a range every month. Each report uses roles posted in the last 120 days and says how many.

Is it actual pay or posted pay?

Posted base pay: what employers are offering for roles like yours right now, which is also what candidates see and anchor on. It leaves out bonus and equity, and every report says so.

Will my employer find out?

No. Your pay is never shown to an employer or anyone else. It's only added to our pay data, in totals of many people, if you tick the box.

How is it different from Sync+?

Sync+ helps you negotiate an offer from a new employer. A Personal Compensation Report is for the conversation with your current employer at review time.

Can it promise me a raise?

No report can. It gives you the market facts and a clear, reasonable ask, which is what makes the conversation go well.
