## What is the posted pay range for a finance manager?

Half of the finance Manager roles that post a yearly range start at $117,300 or more, and half top out at $169,200 or more. The median midpoint, the middle of each range, is $143,400.

These are posted salary ranges, not actual pay. Bonuses, equity and benefits are not included, and a final offer can land anywhere in the range or outside it.

## How does posted pay change from Analyst to VP?

Finance Managers post pay close to procurement Managers, and well above Specialists. The step to Director adds about $51,000 at the median midpoint.

Table: Posted pay by level, live U.S. finance roles, November 2026 (posted salary ranges, not actual pay)
| Level | Roles with a yearly range | Median low | Median high | Median midpoint |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Associate | 63 | $71,900 | $97,000 | $83,000 |
| Analyst | 671 | $70,000 | $98,300 | $84,000 |
| Specialist | 1,327 | $89,600 | $128,900 | $109,500 |
| Manager | 1,271 | $117,300 | $169,200 | $143,400 |
| Director | 756 | $159,000 | $228,800 | $194,700 |

## How many finance postings show pay at all?

67% of live U.S. finance roles post a pay range, or 4,584 roles. The rest leave pay out.

When a posting shows no range, the table above is a fair starting point. Ask the recruiter for the range in your first conversation.

## How much experience do Manager roles ask for?

## How we measured this

We read the pay ranges in live U.S. finance roles that employers post on their own career sites, as of November 15, 2026. Each role is counted once, under its primary field. Only yearly salary ranges between $20,000 and $1.5 million count; hourly rates are left out.

We show the median of the low ends, the high ends and the midpoints for each level. Levels with fewer than 25 posted ranges are left out. We report what postings show now, not change over time.

## What this means for you

- Use the Manager range of $117,300 to $169,200 as your anchor, then adjust for your city, industry and whether you hold a CPA.
- Specialists moving up should expect Manager postings to ask for about 6 years of experience.
- An MBA is named in about 1 in 5 Manager postings and about 1 in 4 at Director level and above.
- About a third of postings do not show pay. Ask early in the process.
