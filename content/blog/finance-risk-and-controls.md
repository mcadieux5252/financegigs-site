## How much of finance hiring involves controls?

A lot more than the job titles suggest. Of the 6,867 live U.S. finance roles we track, 1,683 name internal controls, about 25%. SOX or Sarbanes-Oxley is named in 1,207 roles, about 18%.

Audit appears in 3,385 postings, about half. That covers internal audit roles, but also accountants and controllers who prepare for audits and support them.

Table: Risk and controls work named in live U.S. finance postings, November 2026
| Work named in the posting | Roles naming it | Share of roles |
|---|---|---|
| Audit | 3,385 | 49% |
| Internal controls | 1,683 | 25% |
| SOX | 1,207 | 18% |
| Risk management or enterprise risk | 953 | 14% |
| Third-party or vendor risk | 48 | 1% |

## Which finance levels do the most controls work?

Controls work peaks in the middle of the ladder. Internal controls is named in 29% of Manager roles and 28% of Director roles, against 16% of Analyst roles. SOX follows the same shape, at 23% of Manager roles.

Risk management shifts upward. It appears in 21% of Director roles and 34% of VP roles, against 8% of Analyst roles. At the top, the job moves from running controls to owning the risk picture.

## What does controls work look like day to day?

In most finance teams, controls are built into the close. Reconciliations get reviewed and signed off, journal entries need support, and access to systems is checked. SOX work adds documentation and testing, so a control can be shown to work, not just described.

Managers usually own a set of controls and fix what testing finds. Directors and controllers decide where the risk sits and which controls matter most. That is why internal controls is named most often at those two levels.

## Is third-party risk a big part of finance hiring?

Not yet in postings. Third-party or vendor risk is named in 48 live finance roles, about 1%. In most companies that work sits with procurement, compliance or a dedicated risk team.

Tariffs were this week's topic for our sister brands, but under 25 live finance postings name them, too few to report. We don't report changes over time. These figures are what postings ask for now.

## How we measured this

We counted live U.S. finance roles that employers posted on their own career sites and that we sync into our platform, as of November 18, 2026. A role counts when its posting text names the term, for example "internal control", "SOX" or "Sarbanes", "audit", "risk management", "enterprise risk" or "ERM", and "third-party risk" or "vendor risk". Tags our matching inferred are not counted.

Levels come from each role's title. A level with fewer than 25 roles is left out. See our [Hiring Trends page](/hiring-trends) for the month's full numbers.

## What this means for you

- Put your controls work on your resume in plain words. Internal controls is named in 29% of Manager roles.
- If you have SOX testing or remediation experience, say so. It is named in 23% of Manager roles and 20% of Director roles.
- Aiming for VP? Show how you owned risk, not only controls. Risk management is named in 34% of VP roles.
