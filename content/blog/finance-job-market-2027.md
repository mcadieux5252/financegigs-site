## Which skills and systems are employers asking for most?

Stakeholder management is the skill named most, in 44% of live finance postings, followed by forecasting (37%), reconciliation (34%) and budgeting (32%). Excel is named in 45% of postings, more than any single skill. SAP (20%) and Oracle (13%) lead the ERP systems.

Table: Skills and systems named in live U.S. finance postings, November 2026
| Skill or system | Roles naming it | Share of roles |
|---|---|---|
| Stakeholder management | 2,998 | 44% |
| Forecasting | 2,504 | 37% |
| Reconciliation | 2,298 | 34% |
| Budgeting | 2,172 | 32% |
| Business partnering | 1,460 | 21% |
| GAAP | 1,389 | 20% |
| Excel | 3,078 | 45% |
| SAP | 1,352 | 20% |
| Oracle | 917 | 13% |
| Power BI | 728 | 11% |
| SQL | 680 | 10% |

AI shows up in finance postings more than in procurement or supply chain. About 33% of live finance roles mention AI somewhere, but many of those mentions describe the company or its hiring process. About 4.5% ask the candidate for experience with AI tools, in work like close automation, audit analytics and forecasting.

That is still fewer than 1 in 20 roles. Strong ERP, close and modeling skills decide most hires, and AI fluency is a growing edge on top.

## How flexible is finance work by level?

Finance offers more hybrid work than most fields we track. About 1 in 3 Specialist, Manager and Director roles is hybrid. Fully remote work is about 1 in 10 at most, at any level.

Table: Work arrangement by level, live U.S. finance roles, November 2026
| Level | Roles | Remote | Hybrid | On-site |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Associate | 161 | 2% | 20% | 78% |
| Analyst | 1,410 | 6% | 23% | 71% |
| Specialist | 2,107 | 10% | 32% | 57% |
| Manager | 1,807 | 10% | 31% | 59% |
| Director | 1,108 | 10% | 33% | 57% |
| VP | 229 | 6% | 36% | 58% |

Early-career roles are the most on-site. Analysts and Associates often learn the close and the business in person, so expect more office time at the start.

## Is contract and fractional work growing?

We don't track growth yet, so we won't claim it. 46 of the live finance roles we track are listed as contract, consulting or fractional work.

Employers' own career sites list few true contract roles. Interim controllers and fractional CFOs are mostly placed through networks and specialist firms, so career-site data undercounts them.

## What does finance pay look like going into 2027?

67% of live finance roles post a pay range. The median posted midpoint is $143,400 for Managers and $194,700 for Directors.

## Which credentials matter more as you get senior?

The CPA leads at every level, and its share jumps once you reach Manager. The CFA and an MBA matter most at the top.

Table: Share of live U.S. finance postings naming each credential, by level, November 2026
| Credential | Associate, Analyst, Specialist | Manager | Director and above |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPA | 27% | 43% | 39% |
| MBA | 10% | 19% | 26% |
| CFA | 3% | 4% | 10% |
| CMA | 3% | 5% | 7% |

Base: 3,678 Associate, Analyst and Specialist roles, 1,807 Manager roles and 1,365 Director and above roles.

## How we measured this

We counted live U.S. finance roles that employers post on their own career sites, as of November 15, 2026. Each role is counted once, under its primary field. Skills, systems and credentials are counted when the posting text names them, using whole-word matches.

Pay covers yearly salary ranges between $20,000 and $1.5 million, and we show medians. Any figure built on fewer than 25 roles is left out. We report what postings show now, not change over time.

## What this means for you

- If you plan to lead a team, the CPA is the credential employers name most at Manager level and above.
- Hybrid work is common from Specialist up. Ask about office days early in the process.
- Name your systems and close work plainly in your CV. Employers list ERP and planning tools by name.
- Show one real example of using AI or automation in close, audit or forecasting. Few candidates can.
