## Which certifications show up most in finance postings?

The CPA, by a wide margin. In our November [Hiring Trends](/hiring-trends) count of 6,863 live U.S. finance roles, 2,312 name the CPA, or 33.7%. No other certification reaches 6%.

The CIA, CFA and CMA come next, each in roughly 1 posting in 20. The CISA follows at 3.9%, and the CTP, the treasury credential, at 1%.

Table: Certifications named in live U.S. finance postings, base 6,863 roles, November 15, 2026
| Certification | Roles naming it | Share of roles |
|---|---|---|
| CPA (Certified Public Accountant) | 2,312 | 33.7% |
| CIA (Certified Internal Auditor) | 351 | 5.1% |
| CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst) | 339 | 4.9% |
| CMA (Certified Management Accountant) | 318 | 4.6% |
| CISA (Certified Information Systems Auditor) | 271 | 3.9% |
| PMP (Project Management Professional) | 100 | 1.5% |
| Six Sigma | 89 | 1.3% |
| CTP (Certified Treasury Professional) | 66 | 1% |

These figures match our Hiring Trends page for November.

## How does it change by level?

The CPA is named in 15% of Analyst roles and 43% of Manager roles. It stays near that share at Director, at 44%. The CFA leans the other way, toward the top.

To see levels, we ran the same count on this week's live set of 7,434 U.S. finance roles. Across all 7,434 roles, 38% name at least one of the five certifications in this table or the CTP. The table shows the five levels from Analyst to VP.

## Which certification fits which finance path?

The CPA fits accounting, controller and reporting paths. It is named in 43% of Manager roles and 44% of Director roles.

The CFA is the most senior-leaning credential here, a fit for FP&A, investment and treasury leaders. It is named in 16% of VP roles, against 2% of Analyst roles. No other credential here leans so far toward the top of the ladder.

The CMA shows up most at Director level, in 9% of roles. The CIA and CISA belong to internal and IT audit, at 7% and 5% of Specialist roles. Our [risk and controls post](/blog/finance-risk-and-controls) covers that work in more detail.

## What about treasury, payroll and fraud credentials?

They are niche but real. In this week's live set of 7,434 roles, the Certified Payroll Professional is named in 112 roles, and the Certified Fraud Examiner in 85.

The ACCA, the UK accounting qualification, appears in 36 roles. The CTP for treasury is in the first table above.

The Enrolled Agent credential for tax work is spelled out in only 24 postings, under our 25-role floor, so it is left out. We don't report changes over time. These figures are what postings ask for now.

## How we measured this

We counted live U.S. finance roles that employers posted on their own career sites and that we sync into our platform. The overall table is our November Hiring Trends snapshot, as of November 15, 2026, out of 6,863 roles. The level table and the other-certifications table use the live set as of November 25, 2026, out of 7,434 roles.

A role counts when its posting text names the certification as a whole word in capitals, such as "CPA" or "CISA". Short forms like CMA and CIA can mean other things, so we checked them. Over 95% of postings using either one also spell out the name or list it beside other accounting certifications.

For the other-certifications table we required context: CPP only with "payroll", and CFE only with "fraud" or "audit". The Enrolled Agent was counted only when spelled out, since "EA" has many meanings. Levels come from each role's title, and any level, or certification named in fewer than 25 roles overall, is left out.

## What this means for you

- If you work in accounting or aim to be a controller, the CPA is the credential that opens the most doors. It is named in 43% of Manager roles.
- In FP&A or treasury and aiming for VP, the CFA carries weight. It is named in 16% of VP roles.
- Already holding a CMA, CIA or CISA? Name it clearly, since each sits in a narrower set of roles, around 4% to 5% overall.
